Sortline: Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026. Updated October 30, 2026 to cover the browser version. Publisher: Mission Winning LLC, Texas, USA.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account and no sign-in, on either version of the game, and your level progress and settings (symbols, haptics) are stored only on your own device. Your progress is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

None. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party scripts in either version.

The Android app

The app has no internet access. It works entirely offline and makes no network requests, so nothing about you or your play reaches us or anyone else.

The browser version

The browser version is the same game, played in a web page. Your progress and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. The page downloads its level table when it loads, and the server that hosts it may record ordinary request logs such as an IP address and a timestamp, in the same way any web server does. We do not use those logs to identify you, we do not combine them with anything else, and we do not sell or share them.

Deleting your data

For the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall it. For the browser version, clear the site's data in your browser's settings, or clear your browser storage for that site.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. Questions, or a bug report: email contact@missionwinning.com.
