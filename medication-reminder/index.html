Medication Reminder: Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026. Publisher: Mission Winning LLC, Texas, USA.

What we collect

Nothing. Medication Reminder stores your medicines, reminder schedules and dose history only on your device. The app has no account and no internet permission, so it cannot send this data to us or anyone else.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party crash reporting.

Exports

If you export your history, the file is saved where you choose. We never receive it.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Medication Reminder is a personal record and reminder tool. It does not give medical advice.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. Questions: email contact@missionwinning.com.
