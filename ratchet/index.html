Ratchet: Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026. Publisher: Mission Winning LLC, Texas, USA.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account and no sign-in, on either version of the game, and your level progress and settings (sound, motion) are stored only on your own device. Your progress is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

None. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party scripts in either version.

The browser version

The browser version is the same game, played in a web page. Your progress and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. The page downloads its level table when it loads, and the server that hosts it may record ordinary request logs such as an IP address and a timestamp, in the same way any web server does. We do not use those logs to identify you, we do not combine them with anything else, and we do not sell or share them.

The Android app

The Android app works entirely offline. It has no account, no analytics and no internet permission, so it makes no network requests and there is nothing for it to collect or share. If we ship one, we will say so here and in the store listing, and this page will describe it.

Sound

The game can make a noise — a screw turning, a plate dropping, a bolt landing in a box. The sound is synthesised in the app and plays only if you turn it on. It is off by default, and when it is off the game makes no sound at all.

Deleting your data

For the browser version, clear the site's data in your browser's settings, or clear your browser storage for that site. For the Android app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. Questions, or a bug report: email contact@missionwinning.com.
