Baby Feed & Sleep Log: Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026. Publisher: Mission Winning LLC, Texas, USA.

What we collect

Nothing. Child names, birth dates, feeds, sleep, and diapers are stored only on your device. The app has no account and no internet access, so this log is never sent to Mission Winning LLC or anyone else.

If you export a file, it is saved where you choose. The app does not upload that file.

Advertising, analytics, and purchases

None. This version has no ads and no in-app purchases.

What the log is

A notebook. It does not give medical, sleep, or feeding advice.

Deleting your data

Clear the log in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. An adult writes the log. The app collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. Questions: email contact@missionwinning.com.
